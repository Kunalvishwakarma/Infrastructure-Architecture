# Operational Readiness Assessment

Use this assessment before handing a new or changed infrastructure service to operations.

| Domain | Evidence expected | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Service owner, technical owner, support group and escalation path | Not assessed |
| Documentation | Architecture, dependencies, service catalogue and known limitations | Not assessed |
| Monitoring | Health, capacity, availability, logs, alert thresholds and routing | Not assessed |
| Security | Access model, patching, vulnerability handling, certificates and audit | Not assessed |
| Backup / recovery | Policy, restore test, recovery procedure and owner | Not assessed |
| Change / incident | Change model, incident severity, support hours and vendor contacts | Not assessed |
| Capacity | Baseline, forecast, thresholds and scaling procedure | Not assessed |
| Resilience | Failure modes, DR design, exercise evidence and RTO/RPO | Not assessed |
| Lifecycle | Warranty, licensing, support dates, patch lifecycle and decommission plan | Not assessed |
| Handover | Knowledge transfer, runbooks, acceptance and hypercare exit | Not assessed |

## Readiness decision

- **Ready:** all critical controls have evidence and owners.
- **Ready with actions:** accepted residual risks have owners and deadlines.
- **Not ready:** critical support, security, recovery or ownership gaps remain.

Record decision authority, date, accepted exceptions and review trigger. A completed checklist without evidence is not proof of readiness.
